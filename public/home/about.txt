Designing quantum matter at the nanoscale

Welcome to the Quantum Meta-Matter Laboratory! We use precision nano- and meta-fabrication to tune the fundamental symmetries, electronic properties, quantum phases, and ultrafast responses of quantum matter. By combining designer architectures with optical, terahertz, and nanoscale probes, we aim to uncover new ways of controlling collective behavior in materials and to build platforms where geometry, symmetry, and light become knobs for discovery.
