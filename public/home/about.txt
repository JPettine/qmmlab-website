Designing quantum matter through nanoscale structure

The Quantum Meta-Matter Lab uses precision nano- and meta-material structuring to tune the fundamental symmetries, electronic properties, quantum phases, and ultrafast responses of quantum matter. By combining designer architectures with optical, terahertz, and nanoscale probes, we aim to uncover new ways of controlling collective behavior in materials and to build platforms where geometry, symmetry, and light become practical knobs for discovery.
