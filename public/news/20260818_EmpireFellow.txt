Jake named Simons Empire Faculty Fellow
Jake is honored to be among four Stony Brook faculty members across departments to receive the inaugural Simons Empire Faculty Fellowship. The Fellowship comes with generous startup support from the Simons Foundation, which the QMM Lab will utilize to build up our ultrafast microscopy capabilities and support our talented student researchers!
