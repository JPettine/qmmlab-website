Join the QMM Lab!

We are building a collaborative, hands-on team for students and postdocs excited by quantum materials, nanofabrication, metamaterials, ultrafast lasers, and terahertz spectroscopy. Graduate students can help shape new research directions from the ground up, undergraduates can get involved in experimental and computational projects with meaningful mentorship, and postdoctoral scholars can lead ambitious efforts at the interface of nanoscale structuring and quantum matter. If you are curious, creative, and eager to learn, please email Prof. Pettine with a brief note about your interests, background, and the kinds of questions you would like to explore.
