Join the QMM Lab!

We are building a collaborative team of students and postdocs working on quantum materials, nanofabrication, metamaterials, ultrafast lasers, and terahertz spectroscopy. Graduate students can help shape new research directions from the ground up, undergraduates can get involved in experimental and computational efforts, and postdoctoral scholars can lead ambitious efforts at an exciting frontier of condensed matter physics. If you are curious, creative, and eager to learn, please email Prof. Pettine with a brief note about your research interests and background.
