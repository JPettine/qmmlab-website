High-precision nanofabrication

In the QMM Lab, we push state of the art electron beam lithography techniques to their limit to achieve high-quality matersurface resonances, well-defined symmetries, and superlattice structure down at the natural scale of electron coherence (left image: nanohole and nanostructure arrays, pitch between 300-500 nm and minimum feature sizes below 20 nm). Sometimes we demand too much, and the materials curl up under the stress (right image).
