Symmetry as an ultrafast probe

Terahertz bursts emitted after a laser pulse can reveal hidden symmetry, charge motion, and collective dynamics in quantum materials. This review frames THz emission as a powerful way to watch matter respond on femtosecond timescales while guiding the design of structured materials and next-generation THz systems.
